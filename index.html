Step 1: The Brief (What You Give Larry)

Spring campaign, Blackberry Lemonade. The theme is that spring is
finally here and it's time to get outside and show off all the hard
training you put in during the winter. Earned reward angle. Generate
all social formats (1:1, 9:16, 16:9, 4:5), composites with text
overlay and logo, a video from the hero image, hooks, hashtags by
platform, and claims compliance check. Grok only, use real product cutouts.
That's it. One prompt. Larry parses the request, loads brand context from the Brain skill (voice, claims, products, segments, visual identity), generates hooks, writes platform copy, then hands off to Moe for image generation, compositing, and video.

Step 3: Moe Generates Images — generate_image (x4)
Larry writes the scene prompts. Moe auto-injects the real Blackberry Lemonade product cutout + logo as brand_assets, strips product descriptions from the prompt, and routes to Grok's multi-image edit endpoint. This is what Moe received for each format:
// 1:1 — Instagram Feed
generate_image(
  prompt: "Warm morning desk scene, open laptop, journal, natural
    light through window, a performance drink can on the desk,
    spring greenery visible outside, calm productive energy,
    shallow depth of field"
  provider: "grok"
  style: "lifestyle"
  width: 1080, height: 1080
  brand_assets: [
    "cloudinary/.../blackberry_lemonade.png", // real cutout
    "cloudinary/.../kenetik_logo_gradient_bbl.png" // logo
  ]
)

  width: 1080, height: 1920
